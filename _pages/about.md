I’m a Human–Computer Interaction (HCI) researcher and full-stack web developer with a PhD from Singapore University of Technology and Design ([SUTD](https://www.sutd.edu.sg/)). My work blends academic research with practical system design, ranging from blockchain-based agri-food traceability to physical training systems and mobile-friendly cartograms.

I enjoy building interactive applications. Currently, I’m exploring the intersection of HCI and interactive narrative, focusing on the technical implementation and asset pipelines of game development. I’m also interested in how emerging technologies like AI can push the boundaries of user experience.

**What I Do**

- **Design & Research**: Human-centered experiments and user interface design.
- **Build & Ship**: Full-stack web and mobile development (JavaScript, Python, C#).
- **Collaborate & Lead**: Work across disciplines and cultures; former Chair of [Bangkok ACM SIGCHI Chapter](https://www.facebook.com/bkk.sigchi).
